See & Say

Point your camera, get the words back — read aloud.

If you're new to this — new to losing your sight, new to your phone, or just tired of feeling like tech is talking down to you or over your head — you're not alone, and you're in the right place. This is here for the everyday stuff: cooking, grocery shopping, reading your mail, checking a room before you walk in, reading a sign or menu, even one in another language. Point the camera at whatever's in front of you and ask. That's it.
